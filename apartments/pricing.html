Questions

How does the free first month work?
You add a payment method when you start, but you're not charged for the first 30 days. Build your welcome book, print your QR codes and see guests use it. The first payment only happens on day 30 — and if you cancel from your dashboard before then, you're never charged.
Do my guests need to install an app?
No — and they never will. Your guest scans the QR code and the guide opens straight in their phone's browser, in their own language. No download, no account, no login.
How long does setup take?
An afternoon for your first apartment — most of it is pasting in the things you already send guests by message (Wi-Fi, check-in, house rules). You can publish with the basics and keep adding later; every change is live the moment you save.
Can I use my own logo and colours?
Yes. Add your logo, accent colour and a photo of the apartment — the guest page carries your brand, not ours.
Do I need a demo or a sales call?
No. It's fully self-service — sign up, add your apartment's details yourself, publish, and you're live. There's a live sample you can explore first.
Can I edit my apartment details later?
Anytime. Everything — Wi-Fi, check-out, house rules, recommendations, your branding — is editable from your dashboard, and changes go live instantly. You write it in one language and we translate it into all seven automatically.
Are prices including VAT?
Prices are excluding VAT (+ 21% Belgian VAT where applicable). You'll receive a proper monthly invoice from ABSS Analytics.
Is there a contract or lock-in?
No lock-in — it's month-to-month and you can cancel anytime from your dashboard. No setup fee.
What if I have more than 20 apartments?
The Portfolio plan covers larger operators with volume pricing, bulk import and onboarding help. Get in touch and we'll tailor it.
